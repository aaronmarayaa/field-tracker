# Field Tracker

User-selected direction: sporty, a big stopwatch, and the three running photographs attached in chat.

ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Charcoal and off-white echo the monochrome running photograph; track orange connects the action button to the athletics track.
- A large circular stopwatch is the primary instrument; speed and distance support it as secondary readings.
- The stopwatch ring is a visual seconds indicator, driven by the current elapsed time rather than a decorative animation.
- Impact headings borrow the compact weight of sports signage; familiar sans-serif labels and tabular time digits keep the instrument readable.
- The three supplied photos establish the running context without invented athletes, logos, or external stock sources.
- Tracker photos stay free of text. The trip image uses a dark lower fade so white readings remain legible across all three backgrounds and camera photos.
- Narrow screens use a short contact strip, a central stopwatch, then the route and the location log in that order.
- Medium screens put the stopwatch and route side by side below the photo strip; the start and finish share a full-width section below.
- Wide screens place the photo column, stopwatch, and route map in three deliberate columns.
- Orange marks the primary recording action and the elapsed seconds sweep; white selected units avoid spreading the accent.
- Circles belong to the stopwatch and endpoint markers; the other controls have small corners rather than uniform pill shapes.
- Filled and outlined endpoint markers distinguish the start and finish; play and stop marks represent the recording controls.
- Fixed dark mode follows the supplied dark sports photography and keeps the bright timer and readings prominent.
- Motion is limited to input feedback, the functional loading spinner, and changes in real elapsed time.
- The tracker keeps recording controls on one screen; the history clock opens a separate view of past trips, with no empty navigation destinations.
- The route trades places with Ground covered so the map is encountered first without moving the stopwatch or recording action.
- Street tiles retain their original light colors for readable geographic labels; the surrounding map controls follow the sports interface.
- The white start marker, orange position marker, and outlined route line distinguish location data without decorative motion.
- An empty map waits for a real GPS fix, and a tile-loading failure leaves the trip readings available.
- Android's recording notification uses a white location-status icon so the screen-off service stays visible and offers a way back to Finish.
- Login reuses the supplied starting-line photograph and condensed type to make entry feel part of the same tracker.
- History uses chronological rows with date and distance as scanning anchors. A detail opens a portrait image with the orange GPS shape above Field Tracker, distance, pace, and time.
- The three supplied backdrop thumbnails double as tap targets. An orange outline means the image is selected; camera, save, and share are labeled actions below.
- The history list and detail share a keyboard-dismissable dialog; detail options stack below the image on phones and sit alongside it on wide screens.
